### ABSTRACT

The Sariyer–Kilyos Highway Tunnel Project consists of twin tunnel tubes, each with an excavation diameter of 13.2 m and a total length of 13.7 km, constructed in a densely populated urban area in Istanbul, Türkiye. The project was designed using a hybrid tunneling method, combining conventional tunneling and a Tunnel Boring Machine (TBM). Excavation of both tunnel tubes from the northern portal commenced simultaneously using the New Austrian Tunneling Method (NATM), as the manufacturing period of the TBM required approximately one year. A 4.8 km section of the 7.6 km-long tunnel was planned to be excavated using a large Earth Pressure Balance (EPB) TBM with a diameter of 13.65 m. One of the main challenges of the project was the assembly of this large-diameter TBM within a tunnel section where nearly one-third of the total tunnel length had already been excavated using NATM. Within the scope of the project, nine boreholes with a total drilling length of 909.7 m were carried out to determine the most suitable location for the cavern structure designed for TBM assembly inside the left tunnel tube. Based on these investigations, the cavern was designed in accordance with Eurocode 2, considering an overburden depth of approximately 80 m, an average rock strength of 15 MPa, weathering grades W2–W3, and DD-4 seismic ground motion conditions. The cavern dimensions—30 m in width, 27 m in height, and 75 m in length—were determined based on the TBM length and the required capacity of the portal crane used for TBM assembly. The crane selection was governed by load calculations of the TBM components, with the main drive unit being the heaviest element at around 340 t. This paper presents the challenges encountered and the solutions implemented during the assembly of a large-diameter TBM within a deep cavern structure. The study provides detailed design considerations and step-by-step descriptions of the assembly process, demonstrating how the operation was successfully completed under difficult ground and urban conditions.
