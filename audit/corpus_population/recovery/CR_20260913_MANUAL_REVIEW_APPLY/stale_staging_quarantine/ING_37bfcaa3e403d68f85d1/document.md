# Practical methods applicable for transport of dangerous goods through road tunnels AUSTRIA 

Austrian Road Tunnel Safety Law

- The Road Tunnel Safety Law translates the requirements contained in the EU Directive into Austrian law. 
- It is applicable to all tunnels with a length exceeding 500 m of the primary road network (federal roads). 
- The road tunnel safety law defines the following measures for the transport of dangerous goods:
- Prior to the definition or modification of regulations and requirements regarding the transport of dangerous goods through a tunnel, a risk analysis is to be performed
- To enforce the regulation, appropriate signs indicating alternative routes are to be posted ahead of the last possible exit before the tunnel and at tunnel entrances
- Specific operating measures designed to reduce the risks related to some or all of the vehicles transporting dangerous goods in tunnels are to be checked  (e.g. escorted passage in convoys)

Analysis of DG-transport risk in road tunnel in Austra

- A multistage risk assessment process is applied which is defined in the Guideline RVS 09.03.12
- Risk of DG-transport through road tunnels is assessed separately
- For this purpose the risk model DG-QRAM from OECD/PIARC is applied
- In Austria there are currently two tunnel risk models:

Austrian Tunnel Risk Model (TuRisMo)

OECD/PIARC Model (DG-QRAM)

Analysis of DG-transport risk in road tunnel in Austra

- In 2009 research project was initiated on behalf of the Austrian Ministry of Traffic, Innovation and Technology aiming to establish a complete risk assessment procedurę concerning risk analysis for DG (using DG-QRAM)
- Objectives of the research project
- Expand knowledge on DG transport data in Austria
- Verify assignment of products to DG-QRAM scenarios
- Develop a risk assessment process in line with ADR tunnel regulations
- Define reference criteria for each level of investigation (step-by-step process)

# Practical methods applicable for transport of dangerous goods through road tunnels AUSTRIA 

Stage 1 – Classification Matrix

- Stage 1 allows a preselection of tunnels with low DG transport risk taking into account tunnel type, ventilation system, traffic volume and portion of HGV
- The classification matrix was elaborated by applying DG-QRAM to standardised model tunnels, which are representative for the Austrian road tunnel collective
- Based on a reference value for the Expected Value EV = 0,001 fatalities/year (EV calculated for the tunnel); the approach includes safety margins
- The application of the classification matrix is only admissible, if a set of requirements is fulfilled
- With this approach for appr. 50 % of all Austrian motorway tunnels no measures or further investigations are necessary

# Practical methods applicable for transport of dangerous goods through road tunnels AUSTRIA 

Measures

The current regulation for highways issued by the Federal Ministry for Transport, Innovation and Technology (BMVIT) already stipulates operational measures depending on tunnel length:

- Tunnels with a length between 1,000 m and &lt; 5,000 m require a flashing warning light on the vehicle
- Tunnels with a length of &gt; 5,000 m require a flashing warning light, an escort vehicle following the transport unit and the distribution of transport documents to the personnel of the escort vehicle

Additional operational measures aimed at reducing the transport risk of DG may include:

- Introduction of an overtaking ban
- Introduction of a speed limit
- Installation of an information system
- Installation of a speed control system
