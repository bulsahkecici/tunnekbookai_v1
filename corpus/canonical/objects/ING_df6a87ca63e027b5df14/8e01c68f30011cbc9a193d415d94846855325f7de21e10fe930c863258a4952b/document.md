## Tunneling in marls. The case of Melissi tunnel

The optimal design of tunnel support in soft rock formations, such as marls, depends significantly on the detailed investigation of the geotechnical properties of the rock. The tunnel of Melissi is loacted in a marl environment, characterised by a typical soft rock to hard soil behaviour. In the present paper, the geotechnical behaviour of the marls is analysed and the tunnel support requirements are presented. Finally, construction data are briefly discussed and the encountered tunnel support conditions are compared to those estimated during the design of the tunnel.
