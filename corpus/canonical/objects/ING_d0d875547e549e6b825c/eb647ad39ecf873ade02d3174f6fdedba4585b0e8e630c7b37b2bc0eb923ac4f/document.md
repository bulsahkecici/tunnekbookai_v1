BIM based information management of tunnel shield construction Xiangfeng Duan Department of Civil Engineering, University of Leeds, Leeds LS2 9JT, UK Email: MrSmith0611@outlook.com 1. Abstract: In recent years, people have increased the development and utilization of underground space, and high-tech represented by the shield method has emerged along with the emergence of a large number of tunnel construction. Shield method provides great convenience to tunnel construction, while it is difficult to manage a large amount of information involved in the process due to some problems such as ambiguous information transmission, incomplete information collection and chaotic information management. At present, although BIM is a breakthrough scheme for construction problems, it is rarely applied in construction information management. In order to solve the problem of information management in shield construction and fill the vacancy of BIM in its application, this paper proposes a BIM system with three  main  functions:  information  optimization,  information  collection,  and  information  transmission.  Applying  it  to  information management of shield construction can effectively solve the above-mentioned problems and complete the optimization, collection and distribution of information well. Finally, the application and development trend of BIM system in the field of basic engineering is discussed, in  order  to  provide  engineers  with  better  method  choices,  scientific  guidance  and  suggestions  for  the  development  of  information management of tunnel construction in China. Keywords: shield, BIM, information management, information optimization, information collection, information transfer 2. Introduction: With the population growth and urban expansion, the land population density is increasing, and the contradiction between man and land is becoming more and more prominent(Yu et al., 2020). Compared with the ground, the underground space has the following advantages: (1) Thermal stability: The underground structure can well isolate the surface temperature and maintain a constant underground temperature. (2) High protection: It can cope with various weapons attacks well after taking engineering protection measures. (3) Easy sealing: It is surrounded by rocks, and it is easy to form a closed space for  storing  solid  and  liquid  substances(Zhang  et  al.,  2022). (4)  Internal  environment  controllability:  It  is  isolated  from  the  external environment,  therefore  its  internal  environment  (such  as  light,  temperature,  noise,  etc.)  can  be  easily  controlled.  (5)  Low  energy consumption: The rocks around it become its long-term and stable heat storage and absorption and release system, which has low energy consumption in the process of operation. Thus, people have turned their attention to the underground space for future development and increased its development and utilization in recent decades(Yang et al., 2022). Tunnel is an important part of civil infrastructure, it can greatly shorten the time for people to cross obstacles, and it has more advantages than bridges in ensuring the navigation of large ships and construction in densely populated areas. The construction scale of underground tunnel project is gradually increasing with the continuous development and requirements of underground space(Ju et al., 2021). And shield method is a mainstream tunnel excavation(Liu et al., 2021), it has the following advantages :(1) Fast excavation speed: compared with manual excavation, the shield machine can excavate more than 10 meters per day with its rotating cutters and propulsion system, which greatly improves the efficiency of tunnel excavation. (2) Small impact on ground buildings: It supports the surrounding rocks through the shield shell and segment to prevent the collapse into the tunnel, which makes the shield method can be used for tunnel excavation in the city center and under the highway. (3) Small impact on the environment: The shield method does not require blasting and other operations, and generally only needs to set up a construction site at the shaft  and  foundation  pit,  and  the  construction  noise  and  vibration  are  very  small.  (4)  High  degree  of  mechanization:  Shield  machine advancement, muck excavation, and lining assembly can all be automated and intelligent, so the labor intensity of workers is low in the construction process. (5)Less influence of the external environment: The shield method belongs to the subsurface excavation method, and the construction is carried out in a closed environment, which can be carried out all weather.(Li &amp; Chen, 2012; Lin et al., 2013; Maeda &amp; Kushiyama, 2005; Min et al., 2015; Shang et al., 2004). However, tunnels are generally built in mountainous areas with unstable geological conditions, the operating environment is relatively poor compared with urban buildings, and the designed service life is long, usually about Preprint not peer reviewed

100 years. During the operating life, functional degradation and defects will occur, which requires systematic maintenance(Shi &amp; Li, 2015); in addition, in the process of shield construction, there are a large number of construction machinery and various types of materials and equipment, and workers may encounter risks when using large equipment. Construction information is not summarized in time, and the management process is complicated(Zhou et al., 2018); if the management is not timely and effective, it will cause loss of life and property and low efficiency. BIM (Building Information Modeling), as a comprehensive model integrating building information, was proposed by Autodesk in 2002, which is a subversive innovation and has been applied in the global construction industry(Gholizadeh et al., 2018). It integrates the information required by the design team, the construction unit, the facility operation department and the owner into a 3D model information database through the integration of the data and information models of the building, so that the information can be shared and passed in the whole life cycle process of project planning, operation and maintenance until the end of the building's life cycle. The information base not only contains the state information describing the building and its components, but also the state information of non-component objects (such as space and motion behavior). BIM system has five characteristics: (1) Visualization: It integrates 2D data and finally presents it in the form of 3D, which is convenient for workers to understand the general situation of the project and make plans in the later stage. (2) Coordination: It contains information on all aspects of project design, construction, and maintenance, and coordinates and unifies all aspects of work to facilitate management by the management department. (3) Simulation: it can be combined with VR technology to train workers to operate mechanical equipment so as to reduce accident rate and improve construction efficiency. (4) Optimization: Through systematic collection and classification, it can avoid information repetition and incomplete to improve the accuracy and reliability of information. (5) Graphability: It can not only output the 3D model as the architectural plan, elevation and section, but also output the component diagram. With the help of this 3D model containing construction engineering information, the degree of information integration of construction engineering can be greatly improved, thereby providing a platform for engineering information exchange and sharing for stakeholders of construction projects. At present, the utilization of BIM system in infrastructure is at the basic level, which is backward compared with the application in real estate. And although BIM has been widely used in the process of design and construction internationally, it is still seldom applied in management, especially in the management of construction information. The situation is even less optimistic for China, which still overly dependent on 2D graphics in the construction industry, and only a few enterprises have started to exploit BIM system in construction projects(Xing et al., 2020). This paper aims to design a BIM based system method by utilizing its ability of information integration and transmission, so as to achieve timely and efficient information transmission in tunnel construction and operation as well as maintenance process. 3. Problems of information management As population and urbanization continue to expand, the quantities and complexity of tunnels worldwide has increased. While most tunnel construction projects had been completed safely, a number of incidents happened resulting in delays, cost overruns and sometimes more  serious  consequences  such  as  personal  injury  and  loss  of  life,  which  raises  concerns  about  accidents(Sousa  &amp;  Einstein,  2021). Although the road accident rate showed a significant downward trend during the study period, the tunnel accident rate has remained relatively stable for 10 years(Serrano, 2022). And the severity of tunnel accident is higher than the general road traffic accident(Amundsen &amp; Ranes, 2000), many accidents are closely related to the negligence of information management. This paper will discuss the three main problems of information management in tunnel engineering at present stage: ambiguous information transmission, incomplete information collection and chaotic information management. 3.1 Ambiguous information transmission Not only do workers need to install a large number of components during tunnel construction, such as drainage pipes, lighting fixtures and jet fans, but they also need to ensure that faulty equipment is properly maintained and replaced during maintenance. However, due to the large amount of information, sometimes components cannot be installed in the correct position according to the requirements of the design drawing. For example, during the process of long tunnel construction, the designer usually designs a lighting system because the natural lighting conditions outside the tunnel cannot meet the normal driving conditions and the emergency situation when the vehicle Preprint not peer reviewed lighting system breaks down suddenly. In this lighting system, lamps with a larger amount of luminous flux are arranged at the entrance and exit of the tunnel, while lamps with a smaller amount of luminous flux are installed in the middle of the tunnel. The purpose is to avoid the black hole effect and the white hole effect on the driver and reduce maintenance costs, therefore lamps with different specifications need to be installed in different positions of the tunnel. In addition, a long tunnel may require thousands of lamps to illuminate; if the information of each component cannot be accurately managed, it will not only reduce the efficiency of construction and maintenance, but also lead to accidents(Serrano, 2022). The accuracy of information is also important for the arrangement of tunnel fire extinguishers. As the service time of cables in the tunnel increases, their working performance will gradually decrease, increasing the risk of fire accidents. In  the  event  of  a  short-circuit  accident,  the  cable  will  burn  quickly,  releasing  a  lot  of  heat.  And  the  high  temperature  area  is  mainly concentrated  at  the  top  of  the  tunnel.  If  the  suspension  height  of  the  fire  extinguisher  is  insufficient,  the  flame  cannot  be  effectively extinguished, causing the tunnel lining to fall off due to high temperature(Guo et al., 2022). 3.2 Incomplete information collection In the process of tunnel construction and maintenance, a lot of personnel and equipment information need to be coordinated, such as safety, equipment, work handover, cost, and personnel management information. The lack and incompleteness of information will result in low efficiency of construction, maintenance and management, and even cause personal and property losses. Safety information is crucial, and fire prevention is the key to safety. If a fire occurs in a vehicle while driving on the open road, only the people in the car may be affected by the fire. But if a fire occurs in a vehicle inside the tunnel, it will affects not only the people in the car, but everyone inside the tunnel as well as the firefighters and paramedics who go in to respond, because it's enclosed, the smoke cannot easily escape from the tunnel(Serrano, 2022). In 2012, a serious accidental fire occurred in the tunnel of Snow Mountain, killing 2 people and injuring 34 others (Chen et al., 2019). The loss of tunnel fire caused by lack of safety awareness is much greater than that of road fire, so most tunnel safety measures are related to fire(Lin &amp; Chien, 2021). For another example, in the summer of 2018 in southwest China, a serious water inrush accident occurred in a key traffic tunnel. On the construction site, the underground river suddenly opened, a large amount of groundwater gushed out rapidly, and the river water rushed to the construction site outside the tunnel for equipment and personnel. The accident caused many casualties on the construction site, damaged major production equipment worth tens of millions of dollars, forced production to stop, and seriously affected the production schedule and the safety and work efficiency of construction personnel. The main cause of the accident was that the safety information was not considered comprehensively. The project site is located in limestone strata, the climate of this area belongs to the temperate monsoon climate, humid and rainy, and belongs to the typical Karst landform area. The underground rivers in the Karst area are very developed and will continuously discharge a large amount of water in an instant. Therefore, the construction unit must fully investigate the geological conditions before construction, and take corresponding design measures according to the relevant geological characteristics and geological conditions. In addition, the design of the construction plan is not comprehensive. From the perspective of the severity of the accident, the large-scale equipment on site was flushed out of the tunnel, indicating that such a serious accident was not considered in the construction of the project(Bao, 2019). If the construction unit can take corresponding measures under the premise of mastering all aspects of construction information, it will effectively reduce the occurrence of the above-mentioned accidents. 3.3 Chaotic information management The complex information of tunnel construction, the uncertainty of the functions of the managers and the non-specification of the management objects often increase the management burden, delay the information transmission, which may be catastrophic. For example, in December 2010, the lining part of the Xinqijiaying tunnel collapsed, which was mainly caused by a deep ditch at the upper left of the tunnel. The specific reason is that most of the local workers left construction site due to holidays, and the supporting construction and monitoring activities that should be carried out were put on hold for a long time due to the lack of effective information management and awareness, resulting in excessive deformation of the lining(Zhu et al., 2022). If the personnel management staff can manage and adjust the relevant personnel arrangement in advance according to the holiday time, also ensure that a small number of monitoring personnel remain on the site to meet the daily monitoring needs and transmit feedback to the construction and logistics department, then logistics department according  to  the  information  to  dispatch  the  relevant  construction  materials  to  the  maintenance  workers  arranged  by  the  construction department to deal with problems; in this way that accident obviously can be avoided. On May 2, 2017, a methane explosion occurred in Preprint not peer reviewed the Qishanyan tunnel in Dafang County, Guizhou Province. The huge force caused the tunnel to collapse seriously, about 700m from the entrance, causing 12 deaths and 12 injuries. Cause of the accident: The cumulative length of the tunnel construction reached about 2.4 kilometers, and 100 meters were not completed. The construction workers were so eager to complete the tunnel that they sped up the tunnel excavation and ignored the methane detection, and the management personnel did not supervise the work of the workers, resulting in the lack of advanced detection information of coal seam gas in the tunnel(He et al., 2019) . If the construction party can clarify the functions of the  on-site  management personnel, and the on-site management personnel supervise the workers to conduct pre-construction methane detection according to the construction specifications, such accidents can also be avoided. 4. Construction and functions of BIM system 4.1 Construction of BIM system In order to solve the above-mentioned three main problems, this paper designs a BIM system with three main functions of information optimization, information collection and information transmission (Figure 1). Fig.1. The Process of Establishing A BIM System In China, although BIM has been widely used in construction, most construction personnel still rely on 2D CAD drawings during construction. In order to realize digitization and save costs, the system is based on the existing construction data. The system first collects the construction data of the 2D CAD drawings, and then engineers utilize the Revit software to build a 3D digital model which can presents information in a straightforward manner for a better understanding of the project by all involved personnel through using the same units and grids as the 2D drawings. Finally, an information database including safety, equipment, work handover, cost and personnel management information is established, then the information is subdivided according to departmental functions. 4.2 Information Optimization It is difficult to manage a large amount of information and deal with regular maintenance; in order to ensure that each component in the construction project can be installed in design position and facilitate future maintenance, the system will establish ID code of each component and store it in the cloud database. The ID code has the following characteristics: (1) Brief: It is convenient for construction and maintenance personnel to remember, which reduces the workload of workers and facilitates workers to quickly locate problems in the system. For example, if a fire happen, the staff can immediately locate the fire extinguisher in the 3D model according to the reference number of the fire detector and the corresponding map, which will greatly reduce the loss of people and property caused by the fire. (2) Uniqueness: The combination of numbers and letters ensures the uniqueness of the code and avoids the repetition of the code, which can ensure that the different components can be placed in the exact position according to the design requirements. (3) Priority: In the light of the stress condition, structural characteristics, corrosion resistance requirements, etc., the components are classified into important grades, which can determine key tasks for future maintenance and adjust the inspection frequency to achieve efficient maintenance of the project. Information Composition Safety information is the most important information in engineering, and fire and flood are issues that must be considered in most tunnel construction. For a fire, the information such as the quantities of fire extinguishers, the location and the validity period of the fire extinguishing substances in the bottle are very important, therefore they all need to be collected in the system. In this way, firefighters can quickly obtain the fire protection facilities closest to the fire source from the system when a fire occurs, which can shorten the fire spread time and reduce personnel and property losses. In addition, the location information of the emergency passage in the tunnel can not only be satisfied with the indication signs, but should also be collected in the system to facilitate the on-site rescue command work in a fire. For Preprint not peer reviewed floods, the quantities of drains and amount of regional rainfall will be collected in the system to provide data support for people to evaluate whether the drainage facilities meet the requirements. Secondly, the shield method will inevitably cause a certain degree of land subsidence, so the data of land subsidence should also be mastered to realize real-time monitoring and minimal disturbance to surrounding buildings, especially surrounding key roads and cultural relics. In addition, some accidents occur because workers are not skilled in machine operation, so VR simulation information can also be provided to train workers to improve their proficiency in machinery, which will reduce the accident rate in the process of machinery operation to a certain extent. Due to the highly mechanized characteristics of the shield tunneling method, workers need to focus on the information of equipment which will affect the construction efficiency of the shield tunneling method to a large extent during the construction process such as shield machine cutters, rail muck trucks and cranes, their health status needs to be collected in the system so that maintenance personnel can repair them in time. What is more, work handover information is also crucial to the construction process. Specifically, the owner informs the design department his plan, and then the design department makes a design within the budget and delivers it to the construction department for execution, and finally the construction department delivers the project to the operation department. In addition to the points mentioned above, cost information should also be considered. The construction cost and operation and maintenance cost of the same project are collected and stored in the database to provide data support for the later evaluation  of  the  tunnel  operation.  Finally,  the  employee  information  also  needs  to  be  collected  in  the  system  to  realize  the  tracking management of each worker and ensure that their work tasks are completed according to the requirements, which mainly include the information of mechanical repair teams, auxiliary teams, excavation teams, maintenance and cleaning teams (figure 5). Staff Management 4.4 Information Allocation In order to solve the situation of low construction efficiency and even accidents caused by chaotic information management, this system also has the function of information distribution,  which  will  allocate  information  according  to  the  responsibilities  of  different departments, and it also uses red and blue to indicate whether the different branches of the system are working properly. First, employees must log in to access the system, and each employee has an independent account and a unique password. In order to realize the independent responsibilities of employees in different departments, the system provides users with a certain level of access rights according to the responsibilities of the employees, and the relevant work can only be undertaken by the relevant employees. Compared with the traditional operation and maintenance activities carried out manually by maintenance personnel through various monitoring systems, the centralized and independent management method greatly reduces the risk of management confusion. Finally, each department will report its work overview to the project manager to him know the progress. Moreover, it organizes workers by assigning work to idle workers in time to ensure the normal progress of the construction. Finally, the construction execution department mainly deals with the technical problems in the project and feeds back the completion time of each project process and the daily, weekly and monthly work progress to the manager (Figure. 7). Work Progress Feedback Preprint not peer reviewed Specifically, the logistics department regularly sends inspectors to inspect the tunnel lining, lighting, ventilation, drainage, etc., and then report faults to the system through wireless devices in the form of text descriptions and photos. The operator receives the report in the system and locates them quickly and feeds them back to the construction execution department. The construction execution department then assigns workers to deal with the faults. After the construction and maintenance operations, the workers submit the information of building materials usage and machine wear to the system through wireless devices., and operators in the system feed it back to the logistics department. After receiving the feedback, the logistics department dispatches mechanical maintenance personnel and building materials procurement  personnel  to  repair  the  machinery  and  purchase  building  materials  from  material  suppliers  respectively.  All  personnel assignment information of the logistics and construction execution departments will be transmitted to the personnel department in the system, and the personnel department will supervise the work of workers by tracking the work order process and maintenance results in the system to ensure the normal progress of the project. Workers who are inefficient or fail to meet job requirements are given task reminders or scheduling. 5. Conclusions and Outlook 5.1 Conclusion This paper discusses three major information management problems in shield construction: ambiguous information transmission, fragmented information collection and chaotic information management, and designs a BIM based system to solve the above three problems to effectively optimize, collect and distribute information. While this paper provides a primary design view of the system to validate the proposed framework, more studies must be conducted to test the actual functionality of the system before it can be put into construction. Future research work will further verify this system by applying it to real cases. 5.2 Outlook In China, although BIM system has been widely used in the field of engineering, it still faces many challenges: (1) The construction cost of BIM is 100,000-200,000 yuan/km. In order to reduce the engineering cost, few companies are willing to pay the BIM cost in the project; (2) Basic engineering construction practitioners have a weak understanding of BIM, and most of them are using traditional 2d drawings; (3) Construction activities cannot be completely consistent with the design drawing, therefore how to ensure the validity of information in the whole project life cycle is still a problem. However, the application of BIM by workers in engineering can also bring many advantages :(1) In the economic aspect, workers can reasonably control the quantity of building materials and shorten the project period by simulating the construction process to achieve cost savings, which will generate far more benefits than the construction cost of BIM system; (2) From the perspective of management, the visual and digital features of BIM platform are helpful for operation and staff management. Because the relevant engineering information is collected and sent to the BIM platform, and the staff can grasp it in real time and make timely feedback; (3) In terms of policies, the Chinese government actively promotes the deep combination of digital technology and the construction industry to realize the transformation and upgrading of the industry, thus BIM system is undoubtedly in line with the development background of the times. In conclusion, although BIM system has certain defects at present, its advantages far outweigh its disadvantages and it has great development potential.
