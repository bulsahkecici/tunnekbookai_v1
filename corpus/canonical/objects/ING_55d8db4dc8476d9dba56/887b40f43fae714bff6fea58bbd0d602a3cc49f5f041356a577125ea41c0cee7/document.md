## Essential aspects for a well-functioning site organization

- ■ Clear responsibilities of all project members
- ■ Competent staff on site at all times
- ■ Efficient decision making allowing for a flexible response
- ■ Involvement of the client with competent personnel (project and site management)
- ■ To reduce disputes, decision on excavation and support have to be done concerted between the client and the contractor
- ■ Appropriate project management, site organization and safety management are essential components for successful tunnelling !
