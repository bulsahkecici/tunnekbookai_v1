### Luas Finglas Achieves Ireland’s First BREEAM Infrastructure Excellent Rating

Transport Infrastructure Ireland’s (TII) Green Line extension to Luas Finglas has set a new standard for light rail infrastructure sustainability. The scheme is the first project in the Republic of Ireland to achieve a BREEAM Infrastructure 6 rating as well as the first transport project in the country to reach an Excellent rating at the Strategy and Design stage. Find out more here .
