## More than 100 Entries Received for the ITA Tunnelling Awards 2026 as Judging Begins

### More than 100 Entries Received for the ITA Tunnelling Awards 2026 as Judging Begins

The ITA Tunnelling Awards 2026 have once again demonstrated the strength and diversity of the global tunnelling community, attracting more than 100 projects and initiatives from around the world.

Following the administrative verification process, 81 outstanding entries have been validated and will now move forward to the first phase of evaluation by the Panel of Judges. Submissions that were incomplete or did not meet the eligibility criteria were not included in the judging process.

This year's Awards showcase excellence across the full spectrum of underground infrastructure, innovation and professional achievement. The validated entries span all eight award categories, recognising everything from landmark infrastructure projects and cutting-edge technologies to young professionals and visionary ideas shaping the future of underground space.

The most competitive categories this year are Technical Innovation of the Year and Young Tunneller of the Year , each receiving 18 validated entries . They are followed by Project of the Year (EUR 100–500 million) with 12 entries , while both Major Project of the Year (over EUR 500 million) and Product / Equipment Innovation of the Year feature 10 entries each.

The Awards continue to reflect the international reach of the tunnelling industry. The validated submissions represent 27 countries and territories , highlighting the global commitment to advancing underground engineering. China leads this year's entries with 21 validated submissions , followed by India with 8 and Italy with 6 . Projects have also been submitted from Australia, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, Denmark, Egypt, Germany, Hong Kong, China, Japan, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Peru, the Philippines, Poland, Portugal, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Türkiye, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom, the United States of America and several other countries and territories.

With the evaluation process now underway, the Panel of Judges will assess each submission against the Awards criteria to identify the finalists. The ITA Tunnelling Awards continue to celebrate the projects, technologies and people driving innovation and excellence across the global underground construction industry.

The winners will be announced during the ITA Tunnelling Awards 2026 Ceremony , taking place on 4 November 2026 in Lisbon, Portugal , alongside the CPT 20th Anniversary Conference .

For more information about the 2026 ITA Tunnelling Awards & CPT 20th Anniversary Conference, including the programme, venue and registration details, please visit the conference website.
