Sabir Rustemli received his Ph. D. in Electrical Engineering in 1995. He became an Associate Professor in 1997 and Professor in 2005. He serves as the Head of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering. He also served as Vice Rector for 8 years and Dean for 3 years at Bitlis Eren University

Suat Avcil graduated from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Malatya Inonu University in 2010. In his studies, he focused on lighting technology. He completed his postgraduate studies at the Bitlis Eren University in 2020. He has been working in the field of lighting technology since 2010 and focuses on lighting design

Ahmet Nur received his B. Sc. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Gazi University. He completed his doctorate at Inonu University, Institute of Science and Technology in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. He works in the field of electrical power quality, lighting systems, smart grids, and renewable energy. He is currently working at Bitlis Eren University
