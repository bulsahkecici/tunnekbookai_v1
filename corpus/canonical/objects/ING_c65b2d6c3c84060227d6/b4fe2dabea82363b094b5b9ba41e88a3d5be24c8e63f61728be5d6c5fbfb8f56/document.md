## Small Diameter Tunnelling with Pipe Jacking and Segmental Lining

### Small Diameter Tunnelling with Pipe Jacking and Segmental Lining Featured

Mechanised tunnelling describes the excavation of tunnels using mechanically driven machines, typically Tunnel Boring Machines (TBMs). These solutions have been widely and successfully applied worldwide in recent years for smalldiameter tunnels (internal diameter < 3.8 m). In this range, Pipe Jacking and Segmental Lining are the most common methods, each with different limitations. Small diameters impose particular challenges due to restricted internal space, which significantly constrains access, working conditions, maintenance interventions, emergency response, and overall constructability, thereby amplifying the importance of system reliability and safety-focused design. Currently, there is no International (ISO) Standard for the design and operation of TBMs. A revised version of the current European Norm for tunnelling machinery safety EN 16191:2014 is expected to be published in late 2026. EN 16191 is used throughout Europe and elsewhere around the world. This guideline provides guidance for owners, planners, and designers on the technical and safety feasibility of different tunnelling options, considering recent developments and future regulatory requirements. The normative references cited throughout this guideline are primarily drawn from European practice, with particular emphasis on United Kingdom (e.g. BTS, PJA, CDM) and German (e.g. DAUB, DWA, DIN) regulatory and technical frameworks. While these references are widely recognised and influential, they do not represent the full range of standards and practices applied internationally. Users of this guideline in non- European jurisdictions are therefore advised to consult their national standards and regulations, as well as guidance issued by their respective ITA–AITES Member Nation or Member Association, to ensure that locally applicable requirements, codes of practice, and regulatory obligations are fully satisfied.
