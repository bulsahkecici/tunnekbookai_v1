## FS Engineering: A New Chapter in Global High-Tech Tunnelling

### FS Engineering: A New Chapter in Global High-Tech Tunnelling

A new chapter in the strategic evolution of the FS Group begins with FS Engineering, established to promote integrated, high-tech, and digital engineering worldwide. The new corporate name of Italferr represents the pinnacle of a 40-year journey as the driver of Italian engineering globally. FS Engineering enters the market with this massive legacy, shaped by a forward-looking industrial vision. The company is actively aiming for continuous growth, positioning itself to always remain at the forefront of global infrastructure engineering and digital construction sites. Operating as a cutting-edge tech company and acting as a cross-cutting technical hub, it integrates traditional engineering with Big Data, IoT, Digital Twins, and ESG criteria, prioritising sustainability, asset lifecycle management, intrinsic safety, and advanced workplace security. This evolution aligns its identity with top-tier international players to compete in complex global markets.

Among the several ongoing projects FS Engineering is currently delivering, this deep-rooted expertise actively drives some of the world’s most challenging underground megaprojects. Within the Brenner Base Tunnel (BBT) project, the company leads a joint venture responsible for engineering services related to the railway systems equipment across this strategic 64 km tunnel, managing the comprehensive review and update of the Reference Design.

Along the new Turin–Lyon High-Speed Line, FS Engineering is heavily involved in the Moncenisio Base Tunnel. Partnering with international firms, it manages construction supervision across both the French and Italian sides. Additionally, leading the ISP Joint Venture, it oversees construction supervision for Italian Operational Sites CO1 and CO2.

Overseas, the company’s digital capabilities are deployed on Canada’s Ontario Line. Serving as the PCTL tunnel, ventilation, and fire safety designer for the PTUS package, FS Engineering supports the PNC consortium. Utilising full BIM integration, construction design finalisation (90%) is slated for Summer 2026.

Finally, in the Himalayas, activities are advancing rapidly on India’s Rishikesh–Karnaprayag Line, a 125 km infrastructure featuring 105 km of tunnels. Leading a joint venture, FS Engineering provides design and Project Management Consulting for Packages 1 and 8.

With trusted experience and an innovative digital vision, FS Engineering continues to deliver pioneering subterranean solutions worldwide.
