### Road Tunnel Safety

#### Tunnel Insider Tips

##### When Entering...

- Stay alert!

- Turn on your headlights.

- Obey all traffic signs, signals and pavement markings.

- If you notice smoke near the tunnel entrance, do not enter.

- Stop only in the event of emergencies.

- Listen to the radio for important traffic updates.

- Maintain a safe distance from other vehicles, even if traffic is moving slowly.

- If your car is smoking or burning, never enter a tunnel.

##### In Congested Traffic...

- Listen to the radio for important traffic updates.

- Maintain a safe distance from other vehicles, even if traffic is moving slowly.

- Note the location of fire extinguishers and emergency phones and exits so that you can easily find them if needed.

- Follow the instructions of tunnel officials and/or electronic message signs.

##### If There's a Fire...

- Stop, turn off the engine, and exit your vehicle immediately.

- Leave your keys and all belongings.

- Call 911 from your cell phone or use an emergency phone, if easily accessible, to call for assistance.

- If possible, put out the fire using a fire extinguisher. If not possible, locate the nearest emergency exit and leave the tunnel immediately.

- When exiting, assist others who may need help to exit the tunnel safely.
