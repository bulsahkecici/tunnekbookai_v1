## Guidelines For The Provision Of Refuge Chambers In Tunnels Under Construction

### Guidelines For The Provision Of Refuge Chambers In Tunnels Under Construction

In Helsinki in 2011, International Tunnelling Association Working Group 5 identified the need for guidance on the provision of refuge chambers in tunnels under construction and adopted the following definition. A refuge chamber is a place of relative safety within a shaft or tunnel where workers can be temporarily accommodated and access basic life support services until rescued or it is safe for them to exit the tunnel in accordance with project - specific safety procedures. Refuge chambers should be easily identifiable and readily accessible by tunnellers at risk and by the emergency services. As an integral part of a comprehensive safety and contingency plan, regular maintenance, well-defined training procedures and exercises for the correct use of these chambers are of the utmost importance to ensure the intended use in case of an emergency. This document will be reviewed and revised as necessary in the light of practical experience with the provision of refuge chambers in tunnels under construction. Comment and feedback is welcomed and should be made to the ITA Secretariat through the website www.ita-aites.org. Status of this document: this document is not intended to replace or override national standards. These guidelines provide guidance and recommendations only for good practice. Compliance with the guidelines is voluntary unless they form part of contractual requirements. These guildelines were revised in 2018 to include guidance on the use of refuge chambers when there is a risk of flammable gas being present. Changes were also made in requirements concerning atmospheric monitoring equipment and controls for air conditioning equipment. These guidelines were further revised beginning in 2023, to update them to reflect current practice as well as the revision of EN 16191 and in particular its Annex E. Guidance and recommendations regarding the provision of fresh air bases was added as an Annex A, as not all health & safety regulators permit the use of such devices.
