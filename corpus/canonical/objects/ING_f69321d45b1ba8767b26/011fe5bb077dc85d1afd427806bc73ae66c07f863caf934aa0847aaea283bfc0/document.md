## Mitigation of fire hazards

A fire involving one or more vehicles is the most feared incident in a tunnel. Consequently, tunnels are equipped with numerous items of equipment to mitigate such hazards.

This equipment includes:

- Manual fire extinguishers available to users,

- Ventilation/smoke exhaust systems ,

- Fixed fire-suppression systems.

In addition to this equipment, there are often specific requirements in terms of:

- The reaction to fire of the materials used in the part of the tunnel open to traffic,

- The fire resistance of structures ,

- The fire resistance of equipment in the tunnel.
