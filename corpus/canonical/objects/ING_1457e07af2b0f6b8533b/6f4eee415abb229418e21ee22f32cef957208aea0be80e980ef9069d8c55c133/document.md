## DYKE ZONES

<!-- image -->

Logo

-  Need to be installed in pieces so that they can be carried into the tunnel.
-  Need to be installed using the system available on the TBM.
-  Need to be rigid during advance of TBM to absorb up to 15.000 tons of advance force.
-  Seamless rubber to be installed separately since rubber cannot be welded inside the tunnel.
-  The whole assembly needs to be watertight under up to 11 bar water pressure while providing required flexibility.
-  In Trakya Formation, Sandstone/Mudstone layers cut through dykes (andesite and diabase). Such a geological setting creates a different rock mass medium with variable stiffness.
-  Andesite/Diabase dykes have uniaxial compressive strengths more than 200 MPa.
-  Cherchar Abrasive Index (CAI) is an indication of cutter disc/tools wear rate and in the case of dykes, this index increases up to 4.0 (extremely abrasive).
-  Pink color in geological profile below corresponds to dyke zones faced during excavation.
