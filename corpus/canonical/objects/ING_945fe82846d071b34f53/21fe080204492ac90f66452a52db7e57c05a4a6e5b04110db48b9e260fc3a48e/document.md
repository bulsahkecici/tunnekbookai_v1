## Longitudinal Joint Design For Fibre Reinforced Segmental Tunnel Linings

### Longitudinal Joint Design For Fibre Reinforced Segmental Tunnel Linings

This document supplements ITA guidance publication ‘Twenty Years of FRC [Fibre Reinforced Concrete] Tunnel Segments Practice: Lessons Learnt and Proposed Design Principles’. When the original guidance publication was compiled, the guidance relating to the design of the longitudinal joints was given an elementary description, due to the lack of published research relating to this issue. At the time of publication, the Working Group recognised a need for more research to provide guidance for the design of longitudinal joints, as the consequences of failure of the joints can be catastrophic. Furthermore, current trends towards FRC being used at larger diameter, and towards making linings thinner, necessitate increasing attention by designers to the design for the longitudinal joint. This document provides guidance to assist designers in developing robust designs of the longitudinal joints of fibre reinforced segmental linings. This process is split into two parts: - Determining the joint rotation and resulting contact stress distribution/eccentricity. - Determining the capacity of the joint to resist that stress distribution.
