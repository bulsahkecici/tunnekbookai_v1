## AMAC SCOPE

Türkiye'de farkli sektörler tarafindan projelendirilen ve insa edilen tünellerin büyük gogunlugu Yeni Avusturya Tünel Acma Metodu (NATM) kullanilarak yapilmaktadir. Karayollari Genel Müdürlügü $artnamelerinde kullanilan NATM projelendirme kriterleri, Avusturya $artnameleri temel alinarak hazirlanmistir.

Principals of New Austrian Tunnelling Method (NATM) is generally being applied in the design and construction of the tunnels by different sectors in Turkey. The NATM design criteria used in the Specifications öf the General Directorate of Highways is based on Austrian Specifications.

NATM projelendirme ve yapim sartname kriterlerinde gelistirilen uygulamalar ve édinilen tecrübeler konusunda sektör paydaslarina bilgi aktarimini saglamak amaciyla, Avusturyali uzmanlar tarafindan 31 Mart - 1 Nisan tarihleri arasinda verilecek bu seminerin katilimcilara faydali olmasini dileriz.

Austrian experts will conduct a seminar in order to disseminate the experiences on the modifications in NATM design and construction specifications and on practices developed according to the new specifications between 31th March and 1st April. We wish you a fruitful seminar.
