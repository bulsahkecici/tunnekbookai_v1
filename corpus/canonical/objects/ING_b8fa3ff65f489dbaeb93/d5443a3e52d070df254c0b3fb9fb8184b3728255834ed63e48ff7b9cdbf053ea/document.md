## Tunnelling week

The International Tunnelling and Underground Space Association has organized the first ever Tunnelling Week from 20 to 24 September 2021!

These 5 days of conferences and debates dedicated to the underground construction industry, were the occasion to give an annual overview of the global tunnelling activity, present key figures, and future projects all over the world.

This moment also intended to gather the industry to discuss innovations and advances, but also to deal with current challenges like underground space in sustainable planning, structural use of fiber reinforced concrete precast segments, tunnels refurbishment, Health and Safety issues as well as presentations of the latest ITA WGs and Committees publications.
