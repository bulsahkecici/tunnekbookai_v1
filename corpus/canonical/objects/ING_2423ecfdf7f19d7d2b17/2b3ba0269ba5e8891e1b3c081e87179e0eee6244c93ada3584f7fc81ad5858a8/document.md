## Strategy and General Design

This "Road Tunnels Manual" comprises 4 parts. The first part, Strategy and General Design, considers the strategic and design aspects and comprises the following chapters.

The chapter on Strategic Issues presents the principal strategic elements which any decision maker must take into account before making a decision concerning the selection or design of a tunnel. This chapter is particularly aimed at the decision-makers and tunnel designers of countries that are starting to tackle the construction or major refurbishment of a tunnel.
